1 · Your pay

Singapore has no monthly tax withholding — your payslip only deducts CPF. So take-home = gross − employee CPF, and the tax bill arrives separately.

CPF status
Foreigners on an EP/S Pass don't contribute to CPF, so gross = take-home, and there's no CPF relief.

What this models

  • Resident tax rates, unchanged since YA 2024 (0% to 24% across 12 bands).
  • CPF at 2026 rates: $8,000 Ordinary Wage ceiling, $102,000 total annual wage ceiling, $37,740 CPF Annual Limit.
  • Additional Wage ceiling = $102,000 − Ordinary Wages subject to CPF for the year.
  • The $80,000 personal relief cap, applied after all reliefs are summed.

What it doesn't

  • Non-resident tax (flat 15% on employment income or resident rates, whichever is higher; 24% on most other income).
  • Self-employed CPF/MediSave obligations, and the Not Ordinarily Resident scheme.
  • Course Fees Relief — withdrawn from YA 2026 — and Foreign Domestic Worker Levy Relief, which lapsed after YA 2024.
  • Low-wage CPF phase-in below $750/month is approximated.
